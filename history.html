Wawa Dam

Location: Barangay San Rafael, Rodriguez (Montalban), Rizal, Philippines

Type: Historical and natural attraction

Historical Background

Wawa Dam is a historic water infrastructure site located in the scenic Wawa Gorge in Rodriguez, Rizal. It was built during the American colonial period and became an important part of the water supply system serving Manila.

Surrounded by limestone cliffs and natural scenery, the area later became known as a destination for visitors interested in nature, sightseeing, and outdoor activities.

Why Is It Important?

Wawa Dam represents part of the area's engineering history and connection to the development of the region. It also highlights the natural landscape and tourism potential of Rodriguez, Rizal.
